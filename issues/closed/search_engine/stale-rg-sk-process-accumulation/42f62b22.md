# title
Earlier rg.exe / sk.exe processes are not terminated during continuous typing

# pre-condition
- skim-search.exe running
- Large workspace (searches take several seconds or more)

# steps
Source: FR-107, AC-114, handover §3.3
1. Type 20+ characters quickly into the query
2. Check `tasklist | findstr "rg.exe sk.exe"` during/after typing

# actual result
- PASS: on a generation bump the rg/sk of earlier generations are terminated and reaped synchronously. In ~530 samples at 1 ms in ui_flow, at most 0 processes of earlier generations and at most 2 at once (one rg+sk set of the latest generation).
- Measured with the child process list tracked by the engine instead of tasklist (distinguishes rg of other apps).
- Log: `generation=N cancelled_generation=N-1 killed=[rg:pid(gen ..)]`

# expected result
- At most one rg.exe/sk.exe set (latest generation) alive at the same time
- Evidence: per-generation spawn/terminate records in `ref/actual/logs/skim-search.log`, tasklist output attached
